## Full description

Famy connects families in Egypt with vetted babysitters and home tutors. Browse providers, book sessions, manage addresses and family members, pay through Famy’s supported methods, and message your provider — all in one app.

**For families**

- Sign in with your phone number or supported sign-in options
- Save service addresses and pin them on the map
- Add family members (including children’s ages for childcare and tutoring)
- Book hourly care or tutoring, track booking status, and leave reviews
- Upload payment receipts when required; pay online via Paymob where enabled
- In-app chat and notifications for bookings and messages
- Help, privacy policy, and account deletion inside the app

**For service providers**

- Separate provider mode with onboarding and document upload (including ID verification)
- Manage schedule, jobs, earnings, and customer chat

Famy does not use your camera or microphone. Location is used only while you choose an address on the map.
